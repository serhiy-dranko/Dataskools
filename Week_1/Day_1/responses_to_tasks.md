- [X] Read `04_what_is_a_system.md` and draw or describe a simple system from your daily life

System of the elevator:

1. Inputs:
   - Floor call button
   - floor selection buttons
   - Electricity
     
2. Processes:
   - The elevator receives a request
   - Moves up or down to the selected floor
   - Stops at the correct floor
   - Doors open and close
     
3. Outputs:
   - Passengers are transported between floors
   - Passengers are stuck in the elevator
     
4. Feedback loops:
   - The elevator reacts to new button presses and updates the route
   - If the elevator is overloaded, it will not move
   - If the doors are blocked, they reopen
