## 🧠 Think & Reflect

- [x] Write your answer: *In your own words, what is the difference between a GUI and a terminal? When would you use each?*

    I see the difference like this: a terminal is like talking to a dog. You give a direct command, and the computer responds immediately, like “sit” or “run.” It is all about precise instructions and control.

    A GUI is more like human communication. Instead of giving direct commands, you interact through visuals and presentation, kind of like buying flowers for a girl and saying nice things to create the right impression instead of directly asking for something. It feels more natural and user-friendly.

    I would use the terminal when I need speed, automation, or exact control over tasks and don't have a chance for mistake. I would use a GUI for everyday tasks and when a visual interface makes things easier to understand and use.

- [x] Write your answer: *Where do you already see AI working in your daily life that you didn't notice before?*

    I already notice AI in daily life through social media and recommendations. For example, sometimes I talk about something I want to buy or even think about a product😄, and later I suddenly start seeing it in my Instagram feed or ads. It made me realize how much AI is used to analyze interests, behavior, and online activity to personalize content and recommendations.
