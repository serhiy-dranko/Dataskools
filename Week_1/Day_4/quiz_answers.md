## 💭 Section 5: Thought Questions

*These questions have no multiple choice options. Write your own honest answer.*



**36. In your own words, why do you think showing up consistently matters more than being the smartest person in the room?**
> 
    Small, repeated deposits beat one big flash of brilliance that never returns.
    In data work, nothing is won by one clever insight and everything is won by boring repetition: writing queries, breaking things, fixing them, repeating again.
    Smartest in the room are accumulate ideas but don’t build muscle memory. Meanwhile, someone average who keeps appearing every week slowly becomes smarter. Because they’ve actually done the work enough times that it sticks.

**37. Think about the tools you set up this week — Slack, Zoom, GitHub, Google Calendar, and your folders. Which one felt most unfamiliar and why? What step did you take to get comfortable with it?*

> 
    Slack felt most unfamiliar because it’s not just chat. It has channels, threads, and rules that aren’t obvious at first.
    To get comfortable, I started checking channels daily, replying in threads, and watching how it works.
    GitHub feels easier because I already understand basic repositories and commits.
