- What would you try next?

- How could Lina measure service quality more directly?
  Ask customers for a quick satisfaction score and how long they waited for their drink. Even a simple 1 to 5 rating card at the stand would give real, measurable data.
- Should she test **midweek promos**?
  Yes,  sales data from first two weeks of June  clearly shows Monday and Thursday are the slowest days. A targeted promo could lift those numbers without extra cost.
- Is there any untapped potential in Ginger sales?
  I think Ginger buyers tend to leave feedback more than others, which shows they're engaged customers. Lina could create a customer loyalty card with an extra discount specifically for Ginger to convert these engaged buyers into regulars.
- What happens if she adds a loyalty card for repeat buyers?
  It would reward the customers who already come back consistently visible in the stable weekend numbers and give Lina a lowcost way to increase average visits per customer. Combined with the Ginger discount idea, it could also help grow her weakest flavor organically.
